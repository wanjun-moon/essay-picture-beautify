## Liu et al., RCSCH (Information Processing & Management 64, 2027, 105286)
- Figure 1 (PDF page 2) compares prior unsupervised, supervised and semi-supervised hashing designs with RCSCH. The caption and introduction characterize a semantic cycle connecting calibrated reliable semantics, binary codes, topology and feature selection. Visual separation among (a), (b), (c) is semantically important.
- Figure 2 (PDF page 4) is the overall RCSCH framework. Section 3.2 explains continuous semantic F, binary B, shared P and sparse W; Sections 3.3–3.4 explain entry-wise reliability calibration and label/sample topology. Do not sever the relationship between calibration, hashing and feature selection.
- Figures 3–10 provide robustness, stability, selected-feature percentage, ablation, parameter sensitivity, surrogate iteration values and scalability. Each has distinct experimental controls. Figures 5 and 6 fix the labeled rate at 0.5; Figure 9 concerns a fixed-graph inner optimization stage, not general global convergence.

## He et al., Diffusion Models in Low-Level Vision: A Survey (IEEE TPAMI, 2025)
- Figure 1 (PDF page 2) pairs degraded/low-quality input and enhanced output for different tasks: visual comparability and image provenance are fundamental.
- Figure 2 (PDF page 2) uses overlapping sets to communicate models covering multiple scenarios or modalities: do not replace Venn geometry with mutually exclusive categories.
- Figure 4 (PDF page 3) shows the conceptual forward noise and reverse denoising sequence: preserve direction and meaning of time.
- Figure 5 (PDF page 5) differentiates VAE, flow, GAN and diffusion workflows, with formulas and distinctive objective roles; arrows and topology matter.
- Figure 18 (PDF page 14) is a qualitative comparison across six tasks; preserve same-scene correspondences and source image pixels, and distinguish reference outputs.
